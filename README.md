# Federico Vitale

Full-stack software developer based in Vienna, Austria. Since 2019 I have worked at Namirial on its electronic signature platforms, eSignAnyWhere and Namirial Sign Enterprise, with C#/.NET, TypeScript, React and Angular. I was the technical lead for accessibility, working toward compliance with the European Accessibility Act and EN 301 549.

I hold an MSc in Computer Science from the University of Salerno (110/110 cum laude). I am interested in Human-Computer Interaction, accessibility, applied AI and research on cyberbullying, and I am moving toward project management and research roles.

## Selected work

- **SharErasmus**: one of two project managers of a nine-student team, with full PMBOK-based project documentation.
- **ProSign**: a multiplatform app supporting Deaf people, which led to the ACM paper "ProSign Everywhere" (GOODTECHS 2019, doi:10.1145/3342428.3342695).
- **StApp!**: my MSc thesis, a cross-platform meeting management app with AI summarization and emotion recognition.
- **TheVeil** ([the-veil.it](https://the-veil.it)): an Italian-language editorial brand on folklore, occult history and paranormal culture with more than 78,000 followers across Instagram, TikTok and YouTube. I built its website and content management system with JavaScript, Vite and Firebase, including user accounts with two-factor authentication.
